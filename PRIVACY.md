# Ruler privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Ruler is a Chrome extension that measures the space between elements on a web page and shows the Carbon design tokens they use. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

## What the extension handles

- **The page you turn it on for.** When you click the Ruler icon or press its shortcut, Ruler reads the layout and styles of the elements you hover or pick on that tab, so it can measure them and show their tokens. It only runs on that tab, until you turn it off or the page reloads or navigates.
- **Pinned cards.** When you pin a card, Ruler saves the page's address and the path to the pinned element, so the card comes back the next time you turn Ruler on there.
- **Settings.** Which spec sections and the grid you have switched on.

## Where it is stored

Pinned cards and settings are stored locally in your browser with `chrome.storage.local`. The extension has no server of its own and the developer receives none of this data.

## Who it is shared with

No one. Ruler makes no network requests. When you click **copy** or press **A**, the specs are copied to your clipboard and go only where you paste them.

The extension does not sell data, does not use it for advertising or analytics, and does not track your browsing.

The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Deleting your data

Press Esc to clear the pinned cards on a page, or uninstall the extension to erase all of its stored data.
